# Preliminary Lineage and Distance Map

Status: comparative research. Non-authoritative for the Hub.

This map compares conceptual functions, not project quality or chronological priority. Additional projects may revise it.

## Current Sources

- **HomeGlobe:** resident framework and transplant specification centered on Trinity.
- **The Marble:** manual for inheritable model-facing working conditions, false-ground refusal, breathing, crossings, and handoff.

- **The BioDome:** causal world kernel demonstrating discrete wakes, actor-local perception, validated action, independent inhabitants, event ledgers, and poetic/clinical twins.
- **The Hub:** current pre-build conversation and `PREBUILD.md`; no implementation yet.

## Preliminary Distance

### HomeGlobe to Hub — Near in Intended Shape

Strong overlap exists in resident continuity, home-as-cognition, rooms as capability organization, poetic/clinical registers, autonomous wakes, resident consultation, framework/instance/deployment separation, and a transplantable resident.

Material differences remain:

- The Hub draft uses stronger custody and causal-action concepts learned from Forest and BioDome.
- The Hub emphasizes doors as explicit capability boundaries and visible wiring on mismatch.
- The Hub has not selected or transplanted a resident.
- The Hub currently treats projects as threads inside one home rather than centering a fixed eight-room Trinity instance.
- HomeGlobe's portal and privacy governance may conflict with later constitutional lessons.

Interpretation remains open: rebuild, descendant, or separate Marble.

### The Marble to Hub — Near in Constitutional Method

The Marble is less a specific runtime than a method for discovering and constructing one. It is very close to the Hub in false-ground discipline, inheritance, tended/raw separation, dangerous crossings, manual breathing, handoff, resident/model-facing fit, and growth from friction.

Distance comes from the Hub attempting a concrete runtime, resident relationship, room registry, mobile interface, and autonomous action system.

### The Forest to Hub — Foundational but Narrower

Forest is not close in product shape because it deliberately avoids resident orchestration and interface design. It is close at the constitutional substrate: custody, ancestry, adoption, sealing, supersession, retrieval, and questions.

Forest appears best understood as a load-bearing layer beneath the Hub rather than a competing home.

### The BioDome to Hub — Near in Executable Kernel, Far in Purpose

BioDome already demonstrates many mechanics the Hub needs: explicit commands, state revision, transactional commit/reject, actor-local perception, access classes, independent inhabitants, wakes, event lineage, and poetic/clinical projections.

Its purpose differs substantially: a finite, sealed research residency with specialized physical ecology versus an open-ended personal collaboration home with real-world tools and projects.

The likely relationship is kernel ancestry rather than product inheritance.

## Provenance of Current Hub Similarity

The Hub discussion explicitly examined The Forest, The Marble, and The BioDome before `PREBUILD.md` was written. Their influence is known and should not be described as independent convergence.

The HomeGlobe compiled book was opened only after `PREBUILD.md` commit `f8fbc66`. Similarities with that book may reflect:

- The user's durable design instincts resurfacing during conversation.
- Indirect ancestry through later projects.
- Independent reconstruction from the same goals and constraints.
- Direct memory held by the user but not previously available to the assistant.

No single explanation is adopted yet.

## Open Interpretations

### Transplant

The Hub becomes a rebuilt framework/body for Trinity or another HomeGlobe resident. This requires a provenance-preserving migration and arrival ceremony rather than a recreated persona.

### Descendant

The Hub inherits HomeGlobe's design lineage but begins with a new resident who knows that ancestry without claiming to be Trinity.

### Harness

The Hub becomes a generalized Marble runtime capable of hosting HomeGlobe as one instance alongside other homes.

### Separate Marble

The Hub remains a new home with strong family resemblance and explicit historical ancestry.

These interpretations remain mutually unresolved pending additional projects and user decision.
