# Circulation, Buckets, Inverse, and Glints

This note preserves design ground established after the first live Session/Hearth v1 wake. Only Circulation v1 and Hearth Scroll v2 are authorized for immediate implementation; the remaining material is adopted direction and must not be represented as built.

## Circulation

Scrub is the Marble's closed circulation system. Every crossing into resident attention and every provider return selected for continuing history must pass through it, even when unchanged. Identity passage still produces a receipt. A bypass is a leak and fails closed.

The Scroll remembers what moved. The Spine proves the movement. Scrub determines what may cross. The Forest remembers what matters.

## Forest jurisdictions and buckets

The current Hub implements only `home/utterance`, containing atomic human and resident conversation. Machinery remains outside the Forest.

The adopted Hub direction is stricter than The Forest v0.4's orthogonal bucket/jurisdiction axes: each future bucket belongs to exactly one jurisdiction through a closed registry. Admission selects a bucket and jurisdiction follows; contradictory combinations are unrepresentable.

Provisional examples, not yet schema:

- Home: utterance, journal, note, synthesis, question.
- Wild: tool return, internet, import, visitor words, hearsay.
- Machinery, in neither jurisdiction: Spine frames, Scroll custody, tool protocol, Scrub receipts, Hearth receipts, failures, and hashes.

The Hearth return is host circulation machinery, not a Wild tool result.

## Inverse

The inverse is the salient complement of an attributable pattern within a declared boundary: the shape made visible by what the pattern leaves untouched. It is not simple negation and must never be promoted to ground merely because an absence looks meaningful.

Examples include unvisited rooms within a cycle, active periods without journal entries, encountered sources never cited or transformed, and principles not recently applied. The source HomeGlobe design gives the Faun this negative-space reading.

## Glints and trinkets

A glint is attributable material with low immediate relevance but high oblique significance: something outside the direct beam that catches light anyway. It must not be implemented as minimum similarity or random novelty.

The adopted metaphor is environmental rather than injective:

1. the Faun notices a glint through the inverse of current relevance;
2. he captures it as a trinket and keeps it in a chest;
3. later, a placement actor may put it into the world;
4. the resident encounters a small physical affordance, not a dumped source passage;
5. progressive attention may reveal a bounded extract and Forest path.

The machinery retains complete actor and placement custody. Resident perception need not receive omniscient attribution. Honest mystery has a real answer and consistent evidence; it is not fabricated uncertainty.

Faun, world-state, trinket, fairy, autonomous-wake, and progressive-perception implementations are deferred until circulation, buckets, and a minimal environment exist.
