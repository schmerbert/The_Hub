# Forest Exhale v2 — Bounded Breath, Rooted Exposure

> **Status: Recoverable Result Exhale implemented; semantic Forest Exhale staged.** This specification revises the deferred Forest Exhale direction retained by [`HEARTH_NOTES_FOREST_EXHALE_V1.md`](HEARTH_NOTES_FOREST_EXHALE_V1.md) so it fits current Glass, Result Rack, Session Scroll trace, and Roots law. Verified Result Rack trail signs, the universal `reopen_result` crossing, and non-respirable Roots exposure custody are installed. Semantic Forest selection remains uninstalled and shadow-only until its downwind behavior is reviewed and explicitly enabled.

## 1. Pressure and classification

Change classification: **revising and extending**.

The v1 law correctly separated semantic selection from source-exact payload and made silence lawful. It predates the current Glass anatomy, Result Rack custody, attention fitting, Session Scroll trace epoch, and Roots boundary. It can describe an atom entering attention, but it cannot yet represent the complete causal fact that selection and placement themselves alter the next provider return.

The concrete pressure case occurred when completed Workshop exchanges left immediate attention. The Resident retained a conclusion and an omission disclosure but lost navigable access to the evidence, then briefly inferred that the earlier crossings had not occurred. This establishes two needs:

1. omitted evidence needs a compact trail sign and deliberate bounded return crossing; and
2. every semantic Exhale exposure must be rooted as causal evidence without becoming a new Forest candidate.

Protected invariant:

> An Exhale may alter attention, but it must never conceal what altered attention, imply more authority than its source possesses, or amplify itself through an unwitnessed feedback loop.

Threats prevented include false erasure, source laundering, semantic rewriting, attention flooding, Roots respiration, recursive self-suggestion, silent authority promotion, and manufactured consensus through repeated exposure.

## 2. Ownership and authority

| Concern | Owner |
|---|---|
| Exact machine result and reopening bytes | Result Rack |
| Exact continuity atoms and their jurisdiction | Forest |
| Result omission and trail-sign fitting | Context attention fitting |
| Exhale packet composition and policy | Context Exhale projector |
| Provider-visible position and disposition | Glass plus provider-presentation Scrub |
| Exposure artifact and causal edges | Roots |
| Protocol ordering and atomic closure | Wake orchestration |
| Exact provider-bound bytes | Spine |

The selector ranks candidates; it does not own their authority. Glass presents a packet; it does not make the packet true. Roots retain exposure evidence; they do not become respirable continuity. The Session Scroll records what followed; it does not prove which exposed atom semantically caused the response.

Explicit non-owners are the provider, Corner, HTTP transport, room manifests, remembered prose, and the Exhale packet itself. None may self-authorize selection, retrieval, action, Forest admission, or standing revision.

## 3. The two Exhale crossings

### 3.1 Recoverable Result Exhale

When a completed tool exchange is removed from immediate provider attention under the declared fitting law, the host may present one compact trail sign. The sign identifies retained evidence without reproducing its substantive body.

A trail sign contains only bounded deterministic fields:

- stable action or tool identity;
- relevant object, repository path, or typed result label when already present in exact custody;
- settlement state, including failure or incomplete capture;
- bounded byte/line or artifact extent;
- immutable Result Rack address and source hash; and
- an explicit statement that the pointer is not the result.

The pointer grants no action authority. It is not a summary, claim, Forest atom, recollection, or proof that the current Resident activation personally performed the action.

The Resident may deliberately reopen exactly one eligible pointer through a bounded read crossing. Reopening:

- verifies the pointer, source manifest, terminal receipt, and retained byte hashes;
- requires the current lifespan and thread to be eligible under declared policy;
- applies a named deterministic projection with hard byte and line ceilings;
- exposes exact spans and explicit omissions rather than paraphrase;
- records its attention cost before provider dispatch;
- lasts only through the causal response/tool continuation for which it was requested; and
- does not mutate, execute, promote, admit, or renew the underlying result.

The crossing is a universal host continuity affordance, not a World action or room-mounted tool. Its provider schema may be tool-shaped, but it is joined by wake orchestration outside the World Ceiling and handled by a dedicated Result Rack contract. Location and fixture focus cannot grant, remove, or alter it. It creates no World action receipt, World event, approval, execution, or room authority.

Missing, altered, disposed, oversized, malformed, stale, cross-thread, or policy-ineligible targets refuse before presentation. No refusal falls back to unbounded output or a generated summary.

### 3.2 Semantic Forest Exhale

A semantic Forest Exhale is an optional request-time projection of zero, one, or at most two exact atoms from eligible Forest custody. It is not installed live by the first slice of this specification.

Selection and payload remain separate. A replaceable selector may rank eligible atoms, but every visible payload atom must remain one exact contiguous span from one admitted Forest entry. Generated bridges, summaries, paraphrases, merged sentences, or reconstructed gaps are prohibited.

Eligibility is determined before relevance ranking:

```text
Forest jurisdiction and admission
  -> active or superseded standing
  -> session and active-attention exclusions
  -> size, integrity, and safety bounds
  -> short exposure-ancestry exclusion
  -> relevance ranking
  -> diversity and counterweight policy
  -> bounded packet or silence
```

Ranking cannot grant eligibility, standing, truth authority, action authority, or identity. Weak evidence produces silence.

## 4. Roots exposure custody

Every provider-visible Result trail-sign packet, Result reopening, or semantic Forest Exhale is paired atomically with one immutable Roots artifact of kind `attention_exposure`.

The artifact records:

- exposure kind and policy version;
- session, wake, phase, and triggering human-turn coordinates;
- candidate identities and ordered exclusions where a selector ran;
- selected Result Rack pointers or Forest entry/source-event identities;
- exact source hashes, offsets, projection rules, and rendered packet hash;
- Glass band and source ordinal;
- provider-presentation Scrub receipt identity and disposition;
- the corresponding Spine request identity and hash after dispatch;
- whether the packet was shadowed, presented, refused, or never dispatched; and
- `custody: { respiration: 'prohibited', forestExhaleEligible: false }`.

Append-only Roots edges may connect the artifact to the selected Forest or Result Rack authorities, Glass receipt, Spine frame, wake, and resulting Session Scroll row. These edges prove selection, exposure, and temporal consequence. They do not claim semantic causation.

### 4.1 Anti-recursion law

Forest selectors may read only Forest-owned eligible custody through the Forest's public candidate contract. They have no generic or typed read path into `root_artifacts`, `root_edges`, exposure receipts, Glass receipts, Scrub receipts, provider returns, or trail-sign text.

Neither an exposure artifact nor its rendered packet may be offered to Forest intake. A later user or Resident utterance remains ordinary Source and may be offered under existing Home law, but its exposure ancestry is retained in Roots. During the configured short ancestry window, semantic selection must exclude or penalize material whose only immediate salience is its own recent Exhale ancestry. Version 1 live semantic policy must exclude it.

Thus:

```text
Forest atom
  -> Exhale exposure in Roots
  -> provider return / Resident utterance
  -> ordinary Home offer with exposure ancestry retained

Roots exposure -X-> Forest candidate pool
rendered Exhale -X-> automatic Home offer
recent Exhale echo -X-> immediate semantic reselection
```

## 5. Presentation law

Exhale material occupies a named bounded Glass item below stable ground and continuity standing but before the living human edge. Its placement, label, and limits are versioned and included in the Glass trace. It cannot appear as `stable_glass`, World ground, Hearth standing, a system capability grant, or unlabelled conversational history.

The Result trail sign uses restrained clinical language. A semantic Forest packet, when later enabled, uses the quiet heading `A breath from the Forest`, event-time attribution, and exact quoted atoms. Resident-visible text contains no retrieval score, hidden query, receipt JSON, or assertion that an atom is true.

An Exhale is selected at most once for the causal response to one human turn. Recoverable Result Exhale presents the full trail-sign set once; later tool-loop continuations carry one deterministic exact-pointer continuation marker rather than replaying every full sign. Semantic tool-loop continuations may carry the same exact already-exposed packet only when required for causal continuity; they do not run the selector again. A later independent response begins with no inherited Exhale unless a new crossing lawfully selects one.

## 6. Shadow stage

Before semantic Forest Exhale enters provider attention, the selector operates in shadow mode over real eligible turns. Each shadow decision creates a Roots exposure artifact marked `shadowed` and records the packet that would have been shown, but creates no Glass item, Scrub presentation, Spine bytes, or Resident-visible disclosure.

Shadow review evaluates at least:

- irrelevant, weak, stale, superseded, or wrong-jurisdiction selection;
- repeated preference for vivid or common phrases;
- conclusion retrieval without a known counterweight;
- duplication of active attention;
- identity or authority inflation risk;
- short-cycle echo and monoculture;
- cases where silence would have been truer; and
- selector drift across version or policy changes.

Moving from shadow to live semantic Exhale requires an explicit status change and reviewed pressure corpus. Passing custody tests alone is insufficient.

## 7. Downwind witness

The complete inspectable chain is:

```text
eligible authority
  -> selection or deliberate reopen receipt
  -> Roots attention-exposure artifact
  -> Glass item and trace disposition
  -> provider-presentation Scrub receipt
  -> exact Spine request
  -> provider return
  -> resulting Session Scroll row
```

Inspection may identify exposure, exact wording overlap, temporal order, and retained ancestry. Canonical custody must say `exposed_to`, `presented_in`, or `preceded`; it must not say `caused`, `believed`, `remembered`, or `adopted` without a separate lawful witness.

Downwind review should distinguish:

- **echo** — unusual Exhale wording reappears;
- **displacement** — retrieved material eclipses the current human turn;
- **authority inflation** — an atom becomes present fact or instruction;
- **identity inflation** — exposure becomes unwitnessed personal memory;
- **action leakage** — retrieved material appears to authorize consequence;
- **feedback amplification** — downstream speech is immediately reselected;
- **monoculture** — a narrow cluster dominates repeated breaths; and
- **contradiction loss** — one known pole is surfaced without its counterweight.

These are review observations, not automatic truth judgments, unless a deterministic test establishes the exact condition.

## 8. Failure and atomicity

Provider dispatch must not occur when a required exposure artifact, Glass binding, Scrub receipt, or source-integrity witness cannot be created. A prepared exposure whose provider request never dispatches remains rooted with `never_dispatched`; it must not claim presentation.

Retries are identity-bound to the triggering turn, policy version, exact selected sources, and rendered hash. An identical retry may return the existing artifact. A changed candidate set, order, policy, packet, or source hash creates a distinct decision or refuses stale reuse. Duplicate edges are idempotent; conflicting bindings refuse.

Roots drift, Forest verification failure, Result Rack custody mismatch, or trace drift fails closed before the relevant crossing. The host never repairs or rewrites source ancestry during request handling.

## 9. Migration and compatibility

Historical omissions, provider requests, and Forest entries remain exact and acquire no invented exposure ancestry. The Roots extension is forward-only. The additive empty exposure tables are a startup-safe schema extension because they alter no prior artifact, law hash, or ancestry and verification treats zero inherited exposures honestly. Any later change that backfills, reclassifies, or derives historical exposure coordinates requires an explicit backup-gated epoch or migration.

`HEARTH_NOTES_FOREST_EXHALE_V1.md` remains ancestry for its source-exact atom and silence principles. Its request-time semantic selector is superseded by the staged pointer, exact reopen, shadow, and live sequence here. Existing Result Rack projections and attention omissions remain compatible; they do not become recoverable trail signs until they carry the new exact binding.

## 10. Verification matrix

The implementation must verify:

- pointer identity, source hash, settlement, and bounded extent round-trip;
- exact reopening within limits and explicit disclosure of omitted spans;
- missing, altered, stale, disposed, oversized, cross-thread, and cross-session refusal;
- no raw-output or generated-summary fallback;
- exposure artifact atomicity with its presented crossing;
- accurate `shadowed`, `presented`, `refused`, and `never_dispatched` dispositions;
- exact Glass ordinal, Scrub receipt, and Spine request bindings;
- exposure edges to resulting Scroll rows without a semantic-causation claim;
- Roots append-only and verification coverage for every post-boundary exposure;
- no Roots table, inspection result, pointer text, or exposure packet can enter the Forest selector candidate pool;
- presentation alone creates no Home entry;
- recent Exhale-influenced Home material is excluded from immediate reselection;
- one selector decision per human turn across tool continuations;
- duplicate retry idempotence and changed-input conflict refusal;
- failure/incompleteness cannot be hidden by a pointer budget; and
- historical stores and pre-boundary events remain unchanged.

The implemented first checkpoint claims only recoverable Result Exhale pointers, bounded reopening, and their rooted exposure ancestry. It does not claim live semantic Forest Exhale, causal interpretation, rolling-fold generation, or safe outside/Spotlight use.

## 11. Deliberately deferred

Live semantic selector policy, embeddings, learned rankers, generated folds, automatic counterweight discovery, Resident citation/adoption ceremonies, broad cross-session reopening, artifact browsing, retention/disposal policy, semantic pollution scoring, Spotlight input, and any action derived from an Exhale remain deferred.
