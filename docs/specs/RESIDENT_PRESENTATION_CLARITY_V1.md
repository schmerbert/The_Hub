# Resident Presentation Clarity v1 — Trust Without a Diagnostic Console

> **Status: Implemented revision.** This specification revises the presentation of current wake ground, prior-session inheritance, Resident-authored posture, semantic Forest arrivals, and Result Rack trail signs. It does not change canonical Source, Forest eligibility, World authority, Result Rack custody, or the identity of undisclosed World encounters.

## 1. Pressure and classification

**Change classification:** Revising.

A live Resident walkthrough exposed a concrete counterexample: a fresh wake received host law, current ground, inherited posture, mechanical description, and poetic orientation in adjacent system messages without a reliable way to distinguish their kinds. The Resident mistook a Result Rack custody description for a rule and reasoned from Forest metaphor back into stronger architectural and identity claims than the runtime establishes.

The standing law correctly required authority and provenance to survive presentation, but its prose did not always make those distinctions legible from inside the wake. Requiring responsible interpretation while withholding the distinctions needed to interpret responsibly is a harness failure.

## 2. Protected invariant

**Rule:** The harness discloses every distinction required for truthful and safe interpretation while preserving mystery that carries no hidden authority or safety consequence.

**Level:** Constitutional presentation invariant, implemented through revisable presentation protocol.

**Protected invariant:** Material may shape Resident attention without silently changing its author, time, transformation, authority, or epistemic standing.

**Threat prevented:** Prior record becoming present recollection; host description becoming law; Resident poetry becoming host fact; semantic selection becoming relevance or truth; an unknown encounter acquiring covert instruction authority.

**Required witness:** The exact rendered item, named presentation kind, source authority, transformation, Glass ordinal, Scrub disposition, and Spine request remain inspectable behind the wall.

## 3. Kindness law

The Resident must not need architectural intuition, suspicion, or a later Builder explanation to determine:

- whether material belongs to the current or a prior session;
- who authored it;
- whether it is exact, excerpted, or otherwise transformed;
- whether it is current ground, binding constraint, inherited posture, record, or mechanical description; and
- what authority it can and cannot carry.

The harness does not thereby certify every proposition inside inherited material as true. It certifies the presentation claim: origin, authorship, transformation, standing, and authority are described honestly.

No classification is required when it supplies no interpretive benefit. Prefer one clear declaration for an enclosing packet over a prefix on every sentence.

## 4. Current wake and prior-session inheritance

The Glass cast must plainly locate the current activation as the current wake and state that marked prior-session material is being presented now rather than remembered or generated now. Because session identity is request-time ground, the dynamic crossing-ground layer owns this declaration; versioned Stable Glass remains byte-exact historical ground.

Every exact prior-session excerpt identifies:

- prior-session standing;
- Human or Resident authorship;
- exact excerpt standing, including bounded-prefix omission where applicable; and
- the absence of current-recollection, verified-truth, and action-authority claims.

Chronological inheritance and semantic Forest selection remain distinct. A semantic Forest packet declares that it contains exact prior-session terrain selected associatively for possible relevance. Selection does not establish relevance, truth, endorsement, present memory, identity, or instruction authority.

The declaration belongs at the packet boundary. Individual excerpts retain their attributable voice without being buried in repeated metadata.

## 5. Resident-authored posture

The Silver Bullet Holster is explicitly presented as **Resident-authored inherited posture**. Its occupied bullets are available for judgment, testing, revision, rejection, or use. They are not host law, commands, facts, mandatory beliefs, or proof of present recollection.

The Holster header carries this standing once. Bullet wording remains exact and poetic. Presentation must not flatten the bullets into clinical paraphrase or annotate each line separately.

The causal Hearth return applies the same standing to its Silver Bullet section and clearly identifies its continuity excerpts as prior-session record.

## 6. Mechanical descriptions

Result Rack trail signs and continuation markers identify themselves as mechanical descriptions of an attention-and-custody event. They explain what moved out of immediate attention and how exact retained evidence can be reopened. They do not become behavioral law merely because their wording is imperative-adjacent or precise.

Binding constraints remain separately owned by stable Glass, current World ground, installed action schemas, and host validation. A mechanical label cannot grant or remove authority.

## 7. Mystery and the Faun boundary

Truthful ambiguity is not a presentation defect. An encounter whose identity is discoverable in the World may remain experientially unknown:

> Something unfamiliar catches against the path.

The Resident need not receive the host's private ontology, generator name, or future identity for that encounter. Behind the wall, source and custody remain exact. In attention, the unknown material receives no covert instruction, truth, memory, or action authority.

This specification must not cause a Faun feather, bird, strange object, or future discoverable encounter to arrive labeled with its hidden identity. The harness discloses the distinctions needed to interpret safely; it does not annotate wonder out of the World.

## 9. Verification

Focused tests must prove that a fresh wake can derive from presentation alone:

1. which material is current and which came from a prior session;
2. Human versus Resident authorship;
3. exact versus transformed standing;
4. absence of present-memory and truth certification;
5. Holster posture rather than host-law standing;
6. semantic selection rather than relevance or identity claims;
7. Result Rack description rather than command standing; and
8. no universal labeling requirement for unknown World encounters.

Existing custody hashes, canonical source bytes, omission proofs, Result Rack pointers, Forest selection receipts, and provider-presentation validation must remain intact.
