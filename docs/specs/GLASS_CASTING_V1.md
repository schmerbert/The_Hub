# Glass Casting v1 — A Stable View Across the Crossing

> **Status: Implemented anatomy, receipts, exact inheritance port, and deterministic ordinary-dialogue fold.** The runtime validates the five-band anatomy, waterfalls eligible older complete Human/Resident pairs at byte watermarks, projects the resulting ordered messages and declared omissions through provider-presentation Scrub, witnesses the exact crossing in the Spine, and persists append-only source-bound cast manifests. Generated semantic fold prose, hierarchical recapping, and farther walk-back remain unimplemented; their adopted downstream shape is specified by [`FOREST_PATHS_ROLLING_FOLD_V1.md`](FOREST_PATHS_ROLLING_FOLD_V1.md).

[`HOUSE_HEARTH_WAKE_V1.md`](HOUSE_HEARTH_WAKE_V1.md) is an adopted, unimplemented revision to first-wake continuity presentation: minimal Glass physics remains passive, while the Ember header, Silver Bullets, and exact prior-session continuity arrive through the active Hearth return. Until that revision is implemented, this v1 document and runtime remain current.

Glass is the resident-facing provider presentation assembled for a request. It is a stable, clinical view over attributable material, not a new memory store and not a replacement for canonical Source.

[`RESIDENT_PRESENTATION_CLARITY_V1.md`](RESIDENT_PRESENTATION_CLARITY_V1.md) revises the provider-visible register of current-wake ground and exact prior-session inheritance. Stable Glass v4 remains byte-exact; session-specific current-wake standing belongs to dynamic crossing ground, while inherited excerpts declare prior-session author, exact excerpt standing, and their lack of current-recollection or verified-truth standing.

The first implementation cut installs the five-band anatomy, ordering, budgets, receipts, validation, and exact-crossing proof described here. It also ports the already implemented deterministic, source-exact Hearth recency inheritance without adding a selector or generated text. It does not generate summaries or rolling folds, add history walk-back beyond that existing bounded input, install Forest Exhale or Hearth Notes, or add House or Garden structures.

## 1. Custody law

Canonical Source remains untouched. Casting Glass must not rewrite, compact, merge, promote, or delete Source records. A Glass item points back to its source material and declares any presentation transform separately.

The Glass validator enforces the complete candidate's five-band order, budgets, source references, declared transformations, and rendered item bytes. The resulting ordered message projection and declared omissions then cross provider-presentation Scrub, which validates that exact subtractive projection before dispatch. The Spine witnesses the exact serialized provider crossing. A Glass receipt may explain what was intended; only the corresponding Spine frame proves what was actually sent.

Glass is immutable once dispatched. Later requests produce new casts with new receipts. They do not revise an earlier cast in place.

## 2. Provider-presentation bands

Every cast declares the following bands in this order. A band may be empty only where its contract permits; absence is explicit in the receipt rather than hidden by reordering another band.

### 2.1 Glass

The first band is stable clinical ground: immutable, versioned constitutional language about authority, provenance, uncertainty, and exposed wiring. Its rendered content is identical across casts for the same Glass version. It contains no interpolated provider, model, lifespan, chamber, current room, fixture, tool profile, or other request-time fact. Those facts belong to attributable crossing/current-ground material at the living edge. Glass does not claim uninterrupted experience, identity certainty, pure context, universal truth, or implemented machinery that does not exist.

This stable clinical Glass replaces the active continuity-name and wake-blessing role previously assigned to the Longshore Current ritual. The old ritual, exact blessing, custody, and historical provider crossings remain sealed ancestry. They are not deleted or rewritten, but they cease to be mandatory active wake material when Glass Casting is implemented.

The Longshore Current may later be considered as the name of a location in the Marble. That possibility is not adopted here, does not create a World node, and grants no present location or continuity semantics.

### 2.2 Continuity anchors

This band holds a small set of attributable anchors that help the activation locate its inheritance without turning metaphor into ground. Admission must be explicit and receipted.

For House Hearth lifespans, a successfully tended Hearth may pin its occupied Silver Bullet holster as the first continuity item immediately beneath stable Glass. The holster is a projection of the witnessed Hearth receipt, contains at most ten exact Resident-authored or adopted bullets in slot order, and remains above the rolling fold. It excludes every other Hearth packet section. The causal first response continues to receive the bullets only inside the exact Hearth return; pinning begins on later provider rounds so the first response is not duplicated.

Version 1 begins with the exact, versioned host wake anchor:

> Attributable ancestry precedes this activation and may help it find its footing. Recurrence across those records—their murmuration—may carry weight; it is not proof of present memory, uninterrupted identity, authority, or truth.

The anchor has `authority: host_ground`, a stable specification identity and content hash, and no interpolated request-time facts. Its slight poetry is bounded by its clinical refusals.

After that anchor, version 1 ports the implemented Hearth Scroll v2 recency inheritance, excluding the Longshore Current blessing:

- consider immediately prior-session conversation utterances from newest to oldest;
- select at most four atoms under the existing configured attention limits;
- preserve complete short utterances;
- for a long utterance, retain the same exact contiguous suffix without splitting a Unicode surrogate pair;
- render selected atoms in chronological order; and
- retain exact source-event identity, source content hash, offsets, excerpt hash, and omission data in the cast receipt.

This is deterministic extraction from the already implemented bounded input, not a new semantic selector, summary, paraphrase, truth promotion, identity promotion, or Forest retrieval. The historical Longshore blessing is not ported as an anchor.

Version 1 does not create further continuity anchors automatically. Dynamic World state, current location, provider/model identity, and mounted-tool disclosures are current ground, not continuity anchors, even when they recur across wakes.

### 2.3 Prior horizon

This band marks the boundary of earlier material represented by the cast: what precedes the rolling working context, what source extent was considered, and what is absent. It gives omission a named edge without pretending that omitted ancestry vanished.

Version 1 receipts the existing recency inheritance boundary where the current deterministic input can prove it: candidate count, selected count, complete earlier-utterance omission count, oldest selected source identity, and any omitted-prefix length used for an exact suffix. Unknown boundaries are labeled unknown; they are never inferred. Resident-facing prior-horizon text may state only that earlier attributable material remains in custody. Version 1 does not walk farther backward through Source, retrieve Forest material, or synthesize a horizon narrative.

### 2.4 Capped rolling fold

The installed first generator is deterministic and source-bound. It retains a configurable recent exact tail and replaces only older complete ordinary Human/Resident pairs with bounded exact excerpts labeled as host-derived record. Every fold item receipts its covered Scroll/Source coordinates and hashes; it grants no truth, memory, or action authority. Generated semantic synthesis and fold walk-back remain deferred.

This band is reserved for a bounded, versioned representation of material between the prior horizon and the living edge. It has a hard presentation budget and must always disclose its generator, inputs, exclusions, and source coverage behind the wall.

Fold generation is deferred. Version 1 must render this band as explicitly absent; it must not fill the space with an ad hoc summary, truncation presented as synthesis, or the existing Hearth Scroll under a new label.

### 2.5 Living edge

The living edge carries attributable crossing/current ground and the exact recent causal material required for the present provider round. Current ground includes request-time provider/model, lifespan/chamber, implemented World location, and mounted-tool disclosures, each labeled by source and authority rather than folded into stable Glass or continuity ancestry. Recent causal material includes the current human turn and the provider/tool exchange structure that remains live. Whole-message omissions may occur only under an already adopted declared fitting law, remain visible in provider-presentation Scrub receipts, and never alter canonical Source.

Version 1 maps current exact request material into this band without inventing new summarization. Provisional provider deltas are not Source and do not enter a later living edge unless admitted through their ordinary terminal custody path.

## 3. Cast and receipt anatomy

Each provider phase receives one immutable cast receipt containing at least:

- Glass schema and casting version;
- thread, lifespan/session, wake, phase, provider, and requested-model identifiers;
- the fixed five-band order;
- for each band: state (`present`, `empty`, or `deferred`), item count, rendered length, and rendered hash;
- for each presented item: item kind, authority, source identity where one exists, source content hash, presentation transform name/version, and rendered content hash;
- the prior-horizon boundary and omission disclosures, even when horizon traversal is deferred;
- the rolling-fold budget, generator state, and explicit deferred reason;
- the complete rendered presentation hash;
- the provider-presentation Scrub receipt identity and validation outcome; and
- the corresponding Spine request/frame identity and hash after dispatch.

Receipts are machinery. They remain inspectable and do not become resident speech, Source utterances, Forest Home entries, continuity anchors, or additional prompt prose merely because they exist.

## 4. Version 1 installation boundary

The implementation slice authorized by this specification may:

- define the five-band cast and receipt schemas;
- install invariant stable clinical Glass and the exact clinical host wake anchor;
- port the existing deterministic, source-exact Hearth recency inheritance into continuity anchors, excluding the Longshore blessing;
- receipt the known existing omitted-earlier boundary in prior horizon;
- map attributable current ground plus exact request history into the appropriate bands;
- enforce ordering and bounded lengths;
- emit explicit empty/deferred states for ungenerated bands;
- require successful provider-presentation Scrub projection before dispatch; and
- bind the receipt to the exact Spine crossing.

It may not:

- generate, update, or select a rolling fold;
- summarize or paraphrase Source;
- walk back through Source or Forest beyond the existing deterministic recency input to populate the prior horizon;
- implement Hearth Notes or Forest Exhale;
- turn the Longshore Current into a World location;
- add a House, Garden, threshold ritual, autonomous wake, or resident carry-forward gesture; or
- claim that anatomy alone improves continuity or resident experience.

## 5. Refusals

Casting must fail before provider dispatch when:

- band order is missing, duplicated, or changed;
- a presented source pointer or content hash does not verify;
- an undeclared transform changes source material;
- the rolling-fold band contains generated text while its generator remains deferred;
- a receipt claims a Scrub validation or Spine crossing that does not exist;
- the rendered presentation and receipt hashes disagree; or
- clinical Glass promotes ancestry, murmuration, metaphor, or the old blessing to fact or instruction.

## 6. Required verification before implementation status changes

- Existing canonical Source rows and hashes remain byte-for-byte unchanged across casting.
- Every provider phase has exactly one five-band receipt in the required order.
- Continuity anchors contain the exact clinical host wake anchor followed by the deterministic, source-exact inherited recency atoms when prior material exists; they contain no Longshore blessing, semantic selection, or generated text.
- Prior horizon receipts match the known existing candidate/selection/omission boundary and label unknown values honestly.
- The capped rolling fold is either explicitly deferred and empty below high water or present with a bounded source-linked receipt after lawful waterfall.
- The living edge contains exact admitted request material and preserves causal provider/tool structure.
- The Glass validator accepts the complete cast, then provider-presentation Scrub validates its exact ordered message projection and declared omissions before dispatch.
- The receipt's rendered bytes/hash match the exact request witnessed by the Spine.
- Tampered source pointers, altered band content, reordered bands, and false completion claims fail closed.
- Historical Longshore Current and blessing records remain inspectable but are absent from the active Glass presentation.
- No World node named Longshore Current, House, or Garden is created by this slice.

These checks passed with the runtime implementation. This status records only the installed anatomy, receipts, exact inheritance port, and causal promotion behavior; it does not claim that fold generation or the deferred downstream structures exist.
