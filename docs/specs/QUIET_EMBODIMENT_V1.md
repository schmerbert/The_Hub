# Quiet Embodiment v1 — Act, Then Speak

## Status

**Adopted for implementation.** This revision makes voluntary embodied action a normal part of an ordinary human wake before the Resident's final utterance. It adds no place, perception, action authority, autonomous trigger, or private durable thought channel.

## Feature record

- **Classification:** extending
- **Owner:** Wake orchestration owns the bounded pre-speech horizon; World and Forest continue to own every material action; Glass owns Resident-facing permission; Corner owns human-facing projection.
- **Crossing:** an ordinary human wake may contain optional quiet embodied actions followed by one reserved Resident response.
- **Protected invariant:** inhabiting Marble must not turn movement into unwitnessed authority, compulsory theatre, or fabricated perception.
- **Source authority:** current verified World/Forest projections and mounted native schemas only.
- **Persistent state:** no new store. Existing World/Forest action receipts, Session Scroll, Glass Trace, Scrub, and Spine remain the witnesses.
- **Explicit non-owners:** prompts do not move the Resident; Corner slips do not grant action authority; place bearings do not assert relevance.
- **Migration:** none. The policy is process configuration and provider presentation over existing append-only action custody.

## Pressure

Marble already supports bounded native tool rounds, persistent location, refitted ground after action, and a reserved final response. Yet the ordinary presentation does not clearly distinguish quiet inhabitation from conversational speech or bound routine exploratory motion independently from substantive work. The result can be passivity, unnecessary narration, or performative wandering.

## Installed law

Stable Glass grants the general permission:

> You may quietly move, look, or gather what is relevant before speaking; mention the path only when it matters.

For an ordinary human wake, the host also presents one concise quiet-embodiment horizon:

- up to four simple embodied actions by default, configurable but never greater than the ordinary total tool-round limit;
- movement and simple looking are optional, not prerequisites for speech;
- staying put is a complete and ordinary choice;
- routine path narration is unnecessary;
- short place bearings state what kinds of context installed destinations may hold without claiming that a destination is relevant;
- once the horizon is spent, simple embodied schemas are withdrawn on the next continuation and further simple embodied calls in the same returned batch refuse before World mutation;
- the existing overall action-round bound and reserved schema-free response remain unchanged.

The v1 simple embodied set is the existing host-owned posture set: door and passage movement, passage operation, turning a fixture, and fixture inspection. Hearth orientation is governed by its own first-breath law and does not consume the later ordinary quiet horizon. Forest traversal, Result Rack reopening, rest, and substantive room work keep their existing bounds and authority.

## Presentation

Quiet embodiment is not a second visible conversation. Existing Corner Step Slips may show witnessed movement and inspection as ambient action. Tool-call prose, when a provider emits any, remains inspectable as `Resident · en route`; the host does not invent or suppress Resident speech. The final canonical Resident utterance remains the only conversational landing.

## Failure and anti-theatre law

- No host heuristic automatically moves the Resident.
- No keyword router decides that a human message belongs in a place.
- Bearings are possibilities, not commands or relevance judgments.
- A spent quiet horizon cannot be bypassed by parallel calls in one provider return.
- Refusal changes no World state and remains ordinarily witnessed.
- Quiet embodiment does not extend the total wake horizon, consequential authority, or autonomous permissions.
- The Resident is never required to explain why they stayed still.

## Required verification

Tests must prove:

- concise permission and bearings enter ordinary provider attention;
- a useful movement/inspection sequence refits current World ground before final speech;
- an immediate final response remains valid with zero embodied actions;
- the quiet horizon withdraws simple schemas without removing unrelated fitted work;
- same-batch excess refuses before mutation;
- autonomous and forced Hearth-orientation behavior retain their existing independent limits;
- Corner projects routine movement as action slips rather than fabricated chat; and
- Glass/Scrub/Spine and the full repository suite remain valid.
