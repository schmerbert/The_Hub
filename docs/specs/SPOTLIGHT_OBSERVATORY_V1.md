# Spotlight Observatory v1

> **Status: Adopted production-shell contract.** This specification promotes the isolated Spotlight proposition into a source-coherent Hub room behind the Binder Window. It installs the observatory and its fixtures, but deliberately withholds a traversable Center door until a separately verified read-only observation wire is connected. It installs no brokerage execution, autonomous monitoring, advice, or Robinhood credential handling.

## 1. Adopted shape

Spotlight is not a stage and the Resident is not its subject. It is a sheltered observatory balcony overlooking a wide market landscape. The Resident spots attributable light with a telescope; the room does not place the Resident under a spotlight.

```text
room.center
  -> fixture.binder_window       near pane: Binder-owned portfolio projection
  -> future Spotlight threshold  door withheld in v1

room.spotlight                   installed beyond the glass, not yet traversable
  -> fixture.spotlight_landscape retained broad observation field
  -> fixture.spotlight_telescope deliberate narrow inquiry
  -> fixture.spotlight_archive   recorded past observations and analogues
  -> fixture.spotlight_table     immutable evidence packets and comparison
  -> fixture.spotlight_bell      visible crossed-attention boundary
```

The balcony is an expression of one evidence contract. Its landscape, telescope, archive, table, and bell may be described aesthetically, but expression cannot add sources, tools, freshness, authority, or actions.

## 3. Door law

The v1 World installs `room.spotlight` under `place.hub`, its fixtures, and their containment edges. It installs no `door`, `passage`, movement affordance, socket mount, or reachable Spotlight tool.

The Center may name the absent threshold beside the Binder Window, but it must not present an executable Spotlight exit. No prose, remembered schema, experimental capsule, or installed room node can substitute for a World edge.

A later door revision requires all of the following as one independently verified standing:

1. a host-owned observation adapter whose exposed operations are allowlisted as read-only;
2. proof that no order, cancellation, transfer, account mutation, credential operation, or generic passthrough operation is reachable;
3. bounded provenance, timestamp, staleness, partial-data, hostile-input, retry, and source-disconnect behavior;
4. durable observation/packet custody appropriate to the claims presented;
5. exact Ceiling, mounting, Gateway, Scrub, and receipt wiring with no loose required wire; and
6. an explicit backup-confirmed World migration that adds the door.

The official Robinhood Agentic Trading MCP is not admissible as the v1 telescope lens because it is an execution surface. A future adapter may use another source only after the host proves the complete read-only operation set rather than trusting names or descriptions.

## 4. Telescope law

The telescope is deliberate. Entering or perceiving Spotlight must never initiate a request. In v1 the telescope is capped and performs no network call.

A future telescope inquiry may select one bounded instrument, observation, or discrepancy. It must return an immutable `spotlight-observation.v1` envelope containing:

- source identity and source-owned reference;
- instrument identity without account credentials or private account identifiers;
- observed-at time and received-at time;
- exact declared fields with missing values preserved as missing;
- freshness and completeness declarations;
- explicit `observationalOnly: true` and `financialExecution: false`; and
- a hash over the admitted canonical envelope or exact retained source bytes.

The Hub may normalize representation only where the adapter contract names the transformation. It may not silently calculate causation, advice, confidence, portfolio truth, or market-wide truth.

## 5. Landscape, archive, table, and bell

- **Landscape** composes retained observations already in custody. It does not poll or stream by being viewed.
- **Archive** exposes attributable past observations and recorded analogues without presenting them as current.
- **Table** holds immutable evidence packets joining exact claims, counterevidence, missing-data disclosure, and the next observation worth making.
- **Bell** marks a policy boundary crossing. It is not an alert subscription, conclusion, recommendation, or autonomous wake.

The first production packet mechanism is the deterministic recorded-replay proposition already proven in the experiment, moved into the room package and tested without future-row exposure. Its thresholds are named replaceable policy, not constitutional truth.

## 6. Binder Window composition

The Binder Window's near pane remains Binder-owned. The far pane may truthfully show that Spotlight exists beyond the glass while its lens is capped and its door is withheld. It must not merge Binder portfolio claims with Spotlight market observations or imply that one verifies the other.

When future retained Spotlight material exists, near and far panes retain separate source marks, timestamps, hashes, freshness, and completeness.

## 7. Security and disclosure

Spotlight handles potentially sensitive financial material. V1 stores no credentials, session tokens, account identifiers, holdings, orders, or live market response. Configuration and credentials never enter topology, source control, provider context, fixture prose, or test fixtures.

No Spotlight material enters Forest Home or Wild automatically. No packet is advice. No market movement grants permission to spend. No source-authored text may define a capability or destination.

## 8. Verification

The v1 shell is complete when:

1. a fresh World installs the exact Spotlight node and fixture containment after Binder Window;
2. an existing exact Binder Window World reports a backup-confirmed migration requirement and can append the extension without rewriting ancestry;
3. replay and drift verification reconstruct the exact shell;
4. Center presence names the withheld threshold but offers no Spotlight door;
5. no location can traverse into Spotlight;
6. the production package has an inert `room-installation.v1` manifest and host inspection can report every intentionally capped wire;
7. the deterministic replay packet remains immutable, attributable, future-blind, bounded, and observational-only;
8. malformed and hostile observations refuse without partial custody or invented values;
9. no production Spotlight module performs a network request or imports the experimental host; and
10. the complete Hub suite passes without restarting the Resident or modifying the configured World.

## 9. Deferred

- a verified read-only market observation adapter;
- Robinhood or another provider connection and authentication;
- live or scheduled delivery;
- durable market-observation custody;
- Resident telescope and replay tools mounted through Ceiling/Gateway;
- the Center/Spotlight door and movement events;
- far-pane retained observation composition;
- autonomous bells or wakes;
- recommendations, proposals, approvals, orders, transfers, or any financial mutation; and
- sealed financial custody and provider-disclosure controls.
