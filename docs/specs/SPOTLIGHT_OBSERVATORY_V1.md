# Spotlight Observatory v1

> **Current live-read owner:** [SPOTLIGHT_LIVE_READ_V1.md](SPOTLIGHT_LIVE_READ_V1.md) supersedes the unconditional observation/list/read caps, absent standalone authentication and absent observation custody described in this shell slice. The remaining financial/evidence/replay caps and immutable shell/installation ancestry below still stand. A configured connection and a verified live read are separate facts.

> **Status: Adopted enterable production room, capped-hands, and Robinhood read-adapter contract.** This specification promotes the isolated Spotlight proposition into a source-coherent Hub room behind the Binder Window. It installs the observatory, fixtures, passive Center threshold, complete named hand surface, and a credential-free Robinhood normalization boundary. Traversal grants presence only: every effectful hand remains withheld until its separately verified source, custody, strategy, and approval wires are connected. It installs no brokerage execution, autonomous monitoring, advice, or Robinhood credential handling.

## 1. Adopted shape

Spotlight is not a stage and the Resident is not its subject. It is a sheltered observatory balcony overlooking a wide market landscape. The Resident spots attributable light with a telescope; the room does not place the Resident under a spotlight.

```text
room.center
  -> fixture.binder_window       near pane: Binder-owned portfolio projection
  -> Spotlight threshold         passive bidirectional passage

room.spotlight                   enterable beyond the glass
  -> fixture.spotlight_landscape retained broad observation field
  -> fixture.spotlight_telescope deliberate narrow inquiry
  -> fixture.spotlight_archive   recorded past observations and analogues
  -> fixture.spotlight_table     immutable evidence packets and comparison
  -> fixture.spotlight_bell      visible crossed-attention boundary
```

The balcony is an expression of one evidence contract. Its landscape, telescope, archive, table, and bell may be described aesthetically, but expression cannot add sources, tools, freshness, authority, or actions.

### 2.1 Capped-hands revision

The original shell used an empty tool list to prove that a named fixture could not become financial authority. The concrete pressure case is a room intended to accept several observation and account connectors over time: repeatedly changing the Ceiling, schemas, handlers, and portable-room manifest at the moment each connector arrives would hide the stable hand vocabulary and make an unavailable wire indistinguishable from an unknown future capability.

The protected invariant remains unchanged: naming or mounting a hand cannot grant source, custody, strategy, approval, or financial authority. The revised mechanism installs the complete named hand surface now. A hand whose requirements are absent must return one bounded `withheld` result naming those requirements, perform no network request or mutation, and retain the ordinary World action/Scrub receipt for that refusal. Only the capability-status hand has no external requirement.

The hands revision does not install a credential, provider transport, observation store, strategy, approval, or execution effect. The later threshold revision changes only physical reachability. The Robinhood read adapter is an injectable normalizer, not a connection: a future activation replaces a named missing binding with a verified host-owned transport and custody implementation; it does not add an ambient or generic passthrough tool.

## 3. Threshold law

The World installs `room.spotlight` under `place.hub`, its fixtures, their containment edges, and one passive bidirectional Center/Spotlight door (`door.spotlight`). Crossing it uses the universal `move_through_door` hand and appends the ordinary location event. It has no mutable door-state object, performs no provider request, refresh, custody write, proposal, approval, or brokerage effect, and does not require Robinhood availability.

Location mounts the already-declared Spotlight hand vocabulary. Every hand other than capability inspection remains effectfully withheld while its own bindings are absent. Thus entering the observatory makes the room present without pretending that its telescope is connected.

No prose, remembered schema, experimental capsule, or installed room node can substitute for the exact World passage. Existing Spotlight Worlds gain it only through an explicit backup-confirmed append-only migration. That revision preserves the installed shell event, capped-hands receipt, current lifespan location, World ancestry, and all source/financial gates.

The official Robinhood Agentic Trading MCP is not admissible as a raw telescope lens because it is also an execution surface. Spotlight instead admits a provider-specific adapter with an exact read allowlist: account discovery, portfolio summaries, equity positions, crypto positions, equity quotes, and crypto quotes. The adapter accepts injected operations, emits only admitted `spotlight-observation.v1` envelopes, and exposes no generic call mechanism. Orders, previews, cancellations, exercises, transfers, watchlist changes, scan changes, credentials, and provider-returned identifiers are outside the adapter contract.

Codex-hosted Robinhood authentication and standalone Hub authentication are distinct custody domains. A successful Robinhood call in a Codex task proves that task's configured MCP connection; it does not grant the Hub process a bearer token, session, or callable transport. No credential may be copied from Codex storage into source control, a fixture, World state, or an observation. The Hub source socket remains uninstalled until a separately configured host bridge can inject only the adopted read operations.

Account jurisdiction crosses this boundary only as host-chosen opaque aliases and two policy facts: whether an account is readable and whether Robinhood marks it accessible to the connected agent. Brokerage account numbers, crypto account numbers, account UUIDs, order identifiers, and transport cursors do not cross. Read access to a primary holdings account does not imply execution access; execution remains confined to a separately adopted exact account binding even if Robinhood later reports an account as agent-accessible.

## 4. Telescope law

The telescope is deliberate. Entering or perceiving Spotlight must never initiate a request. In v1 the telescope is capped and performs no network call.

A future telescope inquiry may select one bounded instrument, observation, or discrepancy. It must return an immutable `spotlight-observation.v1` envelope containing:

- source identity and source-owned reference;
- instrument identity without account credentials or private account identifiers;
- observed-at time and received-at time;
- exact declared fields with missing values preserved as missing;
- freshness and completeness declarations;
- explicit `observationalOnly: true` and `financialExecution: false`; and
- a hash over the admitted canonical envelope or exact retained source bytes.

The Hub may normalize representation only where the adapter contract names the transformation. It may not silently calculate causation, advice, confidence, portfolio truth, or market-wide truth.

## 5. Installed hand surface

The installed vocabulary is deliberately provider-neutral:

- `spotlight_capability_status` reports which source, custody, strategy, approval, and execution bindings are present without contacting a provider;
- `spotlight_observation_list`, `spotlight_observation_read`, and `spotlight_observe` address retained and deliberate observations;
- `spotlight_packet_build` and `spotlight_replay` address immutable evidence and future-blind replay;
- `spotlight_trade_propose` and `spotlight_ring_propose` express non-executing proposed intent; and
- `spotlight_trade_execute` and `spotlight_ring_adjust` name financial effects but remain confirmation-class and additionally gated by installed strategy, exact account jurisdiction, custody, and connector authority.

Approval classification is not execution authority. Until a financial approval protocol and effect adapter are explicitly adopted, the two effectful hands refuse before creating an approval or contacting an outside system. No raw provider tool, generic MCP passthrough, credential operation, transfer, deposit, withdrawal, staking, lending, or account-selection hand belongs to Spotlight.

## 6. Landscape, archive, table, and bell

- **Landscape** composes retained observations already in custody. It does not poll or stream by being viewed.
- **Archive** exposes attributable past observations and recorded analogues without presenting them as current.
- **Table** holds immutable evidence packets joining exact claims, counterevidence, missing-data disclosure, and the next observation worth making.
- **Bell** marks a policy boundary crossing. It is not an alert subscription, conclusion, recommendation, or autonomous wake.

The first production packet mechanism is the deterministic recorded-replay proposition already proven in the experiment, moved into the room package and tested without future-row exposure. Its thresholds are named replaceable policy, not constitutional truth.

## 7. Binder Window composition

The Binder Window's near pane remains Binder-owned. The far pane may truthfully show that Spotlight exists beyond the glass and that its passive threshold is open while its lens remains capped. It must not merge Binder portfolio claims with Spotlight market observations or imply that one verifies the other.

When future retained Spotlight material exists, near and far panes retain separate source marks, timestamps, hashes, freshness, and completeness.

## 8. Security and disclosure

Spotlight handles potentially sensitive financial material. V1 stores no credentials, session tokens, provider account identifiers, holdings, orders, or live market response. Its Robinhood adapter is credential-free and transport-injected; it strips provider identifiers before admission and retains nothing itself. Configuration and credentials never enter topology, source control, provider context, fixture prose, or test fixtures.

No Spotlight material enters Forest Home or Wild automatically. No packet is advice. No market movement grants permission to spend. No source-authored text may define a capability or destination.

## 9. Verification

The v1 shell is complete when:

1. a fresh World installs the exact Spotlight node and fixture containment after Binder Window;
2. an existing exact Binder Window World reports a backup-confirmed migration requirement and can append the extension without rewriting ancestry;
3. replay and drift verification reconstruct the exact shell;
4. Center presence offers exactly one `door.spotlight` crossing and Spotlight offers the same door back to Center;
5. crossing changes only the lifespan's World location and mounts/unmounts the room profile without contacting a provider or activating a capped hand;
6. the production package has an inert `room-installation.v1` manifest and host inspection can report every installed schema/handler plus every intentionally capped wire;
7. the deterministic replay packet remains immutable, attributable, future-blind, bounded, and observational-only;
8. malformed and hostile observations refuse without partial custody or invented values;
9. invoking any gated hand performs no network request or financial mutation and returns an attributable missing-requirements witness;
10. no production Spotlight module performs a network request or imports the experimental host;
11. the Robinhood adapter proves an exact read allowlist, opaque account jurisdiction, sensitive-identifier rejection, hostile-return refusal, and zero provider mutation; and
12. the complete Hub suite passes without restarting the Resident or modifying the configured World.
