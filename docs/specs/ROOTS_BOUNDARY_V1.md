# Roots Boundary v1

> **Status: Adopted and implemented for new House Hearth wake packets.** Inherited packets remain exact and are not backfilled with invented custody.

## Promise

Roots retain causal evidence that must remain inspectable but must not become ordinary Resident continuity. The governing law is:

> **Retention does not imply respiration.**

Its inverse is equally binding: nothing enters Resident continuity merely because the Marble retained it.

Roots are an operational database domain, not a Forest jurisdiction, room, actor, or provider-visible context band. Forest Exhale may read Forest-owned custody only; it has no generic source path into `root_*` records.

## Forward boundary

`roots_boundary/v1` records the exact count and final row identity/hash of inherited Hearth receipts. Historical packets remain untouched and may lack rooted custody. Every later Hearth packet is atomically paired with a root artifact and typed wake-packet coordinate.

Fresh operational stores establish the empty boundary automatically. Existing stores cross it with `npm run roots:establish` only after a recoverable backup.

## Wake-packet custody

The rooted artifact retains the exact structured Hearth packet and rendered Markdown; their hashes; session, wake, and Hearth coordinates; all packet-carried Silver Bullet slots, selected atoms, Forest pointers, omissions, and construction receipts; and an explicit `respiration: prohibited` classification.

The Hearth receipt, Session Scroll tool-result row, host-return Scrub receipt, root artifact, and typed wake-packet coordinate commit in one transaction. If the later response Glass cast presents that packet, an append-only `presented_in_glass` edge binds the root artifact to the exact cast receipt and hash. A failed wake may therefore preserve a rooted packet without falsely claiming it reached the provider.

## Storage shape

`root_artifacts` is the immutable content-addressed registry. `root_wake_packets` supplies the structured domain coordinate. `root_edges` connects evidence to external authorities without copying those authorities. Every Roots table rejects update and deletion.

Sensitivity classification is recorded, but v1 does not define the Vault, encryption, operator authorization, reasoning retention, or deletion policy. Those require later law.

## Verification and inspection

Before each wake, verification checks boundary and law hashes, append-only triggers, every post-boundary Hearth/root binding, exact packet and Markdown hashes, and the response-Glass edge for committed wakes. Drift refuses with `roots_drift`.

Wake inspection exposes rooted artifacts and causal edges read-only. It creates neither a mutation path nor a provider injection path.
