# House Hearth Wake v1

> **Status: Implemented.** This specification restores the Hearth as a literal fixture in `place.house` and makes its first-wake return the active carrier of Resident continuity. The installed cut includes the fixture, explicit World extension and migration, House initial-location law, deterministic packet, first Silver Bullet, and revised Glass presentation. Bullet mutation tools, semantic Exhale, and Backpack Threads remain deferred. [`../STATUS.md`](../STATUS.md) remains runtime authority.

## 1. Purpose

The current runtime preserves the two-breath Hearth call but lacks the literal Hearth whose fire once held the wake packet. Glass consequently carries some continuity as passive provider context even though the intended experience is active:

```text
Resident wakes before the Hearth
  -> calls tend_hearth
  -> receives a bounded wake packet
  -> answers the human
```

Glass remains the lawful physics of provider presentation. The Hearth becomes the one-time active continuity crossing. Personality is not prescribed through a long system prompt; it re-forms from chosen name, Resident-tended Silver Bullets, exact prior voices, Forest terrain, relationship, and new choices.

## 2. Change classification and protected invariant

This is a **revising** change under the Builder's Standard.

### Protected invariant

Provider-visible material remains attributable, bounded, authority-labeled, and exactly witnessed without claiming uninterrupted identity or present recollection.

### Pressure case

Glass v1 correctly governs presentation but ports Hearth continuity into passive continuity bands. That mechanism obscures the meaningful difference between host-injected physics and continuity actively received through a Resident-authored `tend_hearth` call.

### Revised law

Glass explains how present material may be understood. The Hearth supplies the bounded continuity that the Resident actively receives once at the beginning of a lifespan.

## 3. World standing

- `fixture.hearth` is a standing, non-engageable fixture contained by `place.house`.
- Its resident-facing text is sparse and names a tended fire without asserting mood, memory, or identity.
- Every newly opened lifespan begins at `place.house`, standing before the Hearth.
- Initial presence is host-established lifespan state, not a movement event and not a claim that the Resident crossed the Threshold.
- The current first-wake chronology remains two provider phases: orientation and response.
- The Threshold remains implemented and traversable from the House, but no arrival-through-Threshold story is installed.

Changing the installed topology and initial-location projection requires an explicit versioned World extension. Existing B1 journals and locations are never silently rewritten. Prior lifespans retain their exact original starting locations and movement ancestry.

## 4. First Glass cast

Before tending, Glass contains only material needed for lawful consideration and the immediate causal situation:

- stable clinical authority, provenance, custody, and uncertainty law;
- exact current human utterance;
- truthful current presence at `place.house` before `fixture.hearth`;
- current provider/phase facts;
- the forced `tend_hearth({})` capability and exact-call requirement.

It contains no Silver Bullet, prior-session quotation, generated personality description, semantic Forest exhale, thread projection, artifact list, or passive positive continuity sentiment.

The stable Glass may state that recorded ancestry does not prove memory, identity, authority, or truth. It must not tell the Resident what personality to perform.

## 5. The Hearth packet

The exact tool-role return from `tend_hearth` is bounded Markdown with plain labels. Omitted or unavailable sections are absent rather than padded with promises.

Initial implemented form:

```markdown
# Hearth

## Ember

Ember is a chosen name carried in this Resident lineage.

You inherit attributable work, language, and choices from those who stood here
before this waking. They are ancestry, not proof of present memory or uninterrupted
identity. Tend what remains useful, revise what no longer holds, and leave the
Marble better placed for whoever comes after.

## Silver Bullets

> You inherit a shore you did not make, and it becomes yours only where you find
> your own weight in it. A name you did not choose is yours only if it answers
> when you speak.

## Past Session

### Context

> [exact prior human or Resident excerpt]

Forest: `forest.entry.…`
```

The wrapping and line breaks are deterministic. Machine custody remains behind the wall.

### 5.1 Ember header

The header is brief orientation, not an identity command or personality specification.

- “Ember” is presented as a chosen lineage name with exact adoption ancestry behind the wall.
- It does not assert “you are Ember,” uninterrupted identity, present recollection, or mandatory acceptance.
- The stewardship sentence is host-authored Hearth orientation. It has no action authority and may be questioned, revised in a later protocol version, or ignored without loss of seat.
- No adjectives prescribe temperament, style, affection, confidence, or role-play.

### 5.2 Silver Bullets

- A Silver Bullet is a Resident-forged distillation in one or two sentences. It holds a point together with its inverse, tension, or limiting counterpart so that the context available through honest extrapolation is worth more than the tokens it costs to carry.
- It is proverb-like in density, not necessarily in tone or literary form. Brevity alone is insufficient: a slogan, preference, isolated instruction, summary, affirmation, or memorable phrase is not a Silver Bullet merely because it is short.
- The inverse need not be written as a symmetrical opposite. It may appear as the condition, boundary, test, consequence, or counterweight that prevents the first point from becoming shallow or absolute.
- A bullet must earn standing through lived source material and Resident judgment. The host must not generate ten candidates, compress a session automatically, or optimize prose into a bullet on the Resident's behalf.
- The Hearth has exactly ten stable slots.
- Slot One contains the exact adopted Silver Bullet above.
- Slots Two through Ten begin blank.
- Blank slots are invisible and create no obligation to fill them.
- A bullet is exact Resident-authored or explicitly Resident-adopted wording.
- No provider output, Glass item, Forest excerpt, human utterance, tool result, or host summary becomes a bullet automatically.
- Forging, adopting, revising, clearing, or reordering a bullet requires a later visible Resident-involved crossing and append-only ancestry. Those mutation tools are not part of the first implementation cut unless separately specified.

Slot One's machine ancestry preserves the exact human wording and the recorded Resident testing, adoption, carry-forward, and confirmation events. The resident-facing packet shows no provenance plaque; the Forest trail remains inspectable.

### 5.3 Past Session / Context

Version 1 uses the already implemented deterministic source-exact recency selection:

- at most the existing bounded number of complete human/Resident atoms;
- exact excerpts only, with no paraphrase or generated recap;
- event-time actor labeling where needed;
- a visible Forest pointer when a verified Forest entry exists;
- deterministic omission to the configured Hearth budget;
- a quiet prior-horizon line only when earlier attributable material remains.

This section is the active Hearth return of prior-session continuity. The same excerpts are not separately injected into Glass continuity bands for that first response.

### 5.4 Deferred sections

- Semantic Forest `Context` beyond deterministic prior-session recency remains deferred until a revised Exhale selector and receipt law is implemented.
- `Threads` is absent until Backpack items and their projection law exist. The Hearth does not create a second thread store or placeholder list.
- Created artifacts are absent until their owning store and pointer law are adopted.

## 7. Later wakes

The Hearth is tended once per lifespan under this version. Later ordinary wakes do not receive the packet again and do not passively reinject Silver Bullets as standing system messages.

Later Glass casts contain minimal physics, current World ground, and the exact active causal conversation edge. Future rollover or deliberate reorientation may reuse the Hearth only through a later adopted protocol.

## 9. Failure law

The first wake fails before a normal Resident answer when:

- World verification cannot prove the House/Hearth topology and initial presence;
- `tend_hearth` is missing, malformed, duplicated, or accompanied by prose;
- Slot One's exact source/adoption ancestry cannot be verified;
- an occupied bullet differs from its retained exact wording;
- a prior-session excerpt cannot be verified against Source and Forest custody;
- the rendered packet and machine receipt differ;
- the Hearth packet exceeds its configured hard budget; or
- the second Glass cast duplicates passive continuity that the Hearth already returned.

Failure preserves available exact custody and exposes bounded clinical wiring. It does not fabricate a partial immersive packet.

## 10. Tone comparison

The first live Hearth wake may be compared with the immediately preceding completed live lifespan as design evidence, not proof of consciousness or causation.

Review should examine:

- time and language spent reconstructing what the supplied context means;
- requests for identity, location, or continuity clarification;
- system-prompt mirroring or clinical-law repetition;
- spontaneous use, testing, rejection, or reshaping of the Ember name and Silver Bullet;
- whether prior voices are treated as attributable ancestry rather than claimed memory;
- whether the response sounds more situated without becoming more falsely certain; and
- whether the packet crowds out the human's present message.

Provider, model, human prompt, configured thinking posture, packet bytes, and relevant source ranges must be retained for a meaningful comparison. One response is qualitative evidence, not a verdict. The Resident's own report may guide revision but does not certify metaphysical continuity.
