# Corner Place-First v1 — The World at the Living Edge

> **Status: Implemented revision.** This specification revises the shared browser/Electron Corner renderer. It does not change World authority, wake orchestration, provider presentation, Source custody, approval law, desktop lifecycle, or any installed capability.

## 1. Pressure and classification

**Change classification:** Revising.

Corner's original compact-presence, conversation-rail, and inspection-tray grammar remains sound. The Hub has since gained a material World, room-specific hands, live causal steps, recoverable evidence, Forest traversal, and installation witnesses. The renderer still presents these primarily as small operational labels, chat bubbles, and a diagnostic panel. The World therefore exists in runtime law without yet organizing the Resident-facing experience.

The concrete pressure is not cosmetic. Operational metadata competes with experiential ground; large blank regions coexist with narrow message cards; raw technical inspection dominates the secondary surface; and changing location does not materially change the Corner's atmosphere or the legibility of what is within reach.

## 2. Protected invariant

**Rule:** The Corner presents current place and material reach before implementation machinery, while preserving exact provenance, authority, and Builder inspectability behind an explicit boundary.

**Level:** Protocol law implementing the existing Resident-kindness invariant.

**Protected invariant:** Resident-facing immersion must not falsify location, capability, happenedness, custody, or uncertainty.

**Threat prevented:** A decorative room selector inventing travel; atmosphere implying unavailable perception or authority; Builder diagnostics becoming Resident experiential ground; operational state overwhelming the living conversation; visual mystery hiding a consequential distinction.

**Required witness:** Every displayed location, fixture, action, approval, wake step, result, and inspection value remains derived from the existing same-origin API, current committed/runtime projection, or provisional event contract. Visual treatment grants no authority.

## 3. Product hierarchy

The expanded Corner follows this order:

1. **Resident presence** — provisional or adopted name, explicit host status, and a quiet compact/expanded identity surface.
2. **Current place** — current World ground expressed as one restrained atmospheric band, not a global navigation claim.
3. **Living edge** — human ground, causal steps, and final Resident speech remain the central working surface.
4. **Within reach** — a bounded contextual rail derived from installed current capabilities, fixtures, routes, pending work, or evidence already available through runtime projections.
5. **Behind the wall** — wake inspection, World installation evidence, hashes, sockets, raw projections, and approvals remain deliberately accessible without sharing the Resident-facing register.

The Corner is not a literal map, game scene, or room simulator. Place is communicated through hierarchy, copy, density, subtle color/texture, and the organization of real affordances.

## 4. Location truth

Room-aware presentation is derived only from current World or health projection. It may name and visually distinguish installed locations such as House, Garden, Forest, Workshop, and Spotlight when current runtime truth supplies that location. It must not render location tabs as movement controls unless a real installed movement action is being invoked through its owning crossing.

Atmosphere may change by verified place, but it cannot invent weather, inhabitants, fixtures, observations, work, financial state, or available hands. Unknown or unsupported locations receive a truthful neutral treatment rather than guessed prose.

The Faun and future discoverable unknowns retain the mystery boundary in [`RESIDENT_PRESENTATION_CLARITY_V1.md`](RESIDENT_PRESENTATION_CLARITY_V1.md). Corner presentation cannot reveal a hidden ontology that Resident attention has not lawfully discovered.

## 5. Living conversation

The active lifespan remains the only ordinary conversation rail. User ground, final Resident testimony, host failure, provisional thinking, en-route speech, actions, approvals, and results retain their existing causal and authority distinctions.

The revision improves reading measure, spacing, headings, quotation treatment, and long-response flow without inserting untrusted HTML. Exact content remains exact. Any lightweight formatting is constructed through bounded safe DOM operations and must not reinterpret a source string as executable markup.

Prior-session record, mechanical description, current ground, and Resident-authored posture may receive distinct quiet treatments only when the existing content or presentation metadata establishes that kind. Styling cannot infer a kind from poetic vocabulary alone.

## 6. Contextual rail

The rail answers two questions:

- What is presently established around this conversation?
- What is materially within reach now?

Its items are projections, not new controls by default. A displayed affordance becomes actionable only when it is already installed and the existing renderer has a lawful invocation path. The first slice may remain descriptive rather than creating new tool or movement entry points.

Pending approvals, active work, recoverable evidence, room profile, and fixture presence may appear when their existing projections support them. Absent information remains absent rather than replaced with sample content.

## 7. Builder boundary

The existing wake inspection, Marble Inspector, approval review, hashes, custody, sockets, and raw World data remain complete. They move behind one explicit Builder-facing affordance and retain a denser clinical register. Opening this surface performs no Resident action and does not enter provider attention.

This is a presentation boundary, not secrecy: operational evidence stays fully inspectable by the Builder while no longer defining the default emotional register of the Corner.

## 8. Desktop and responsive standing

- Electron compact mode remains exactly 96 by 96 CSS pixels and does not imply sleep, presence, or activity.
- Expanded Electron remains 980 by 680 unless clamped by the existing desktop controller.
- Browser and desktop use the same HTML, CSS, JavaScript, API, event stream, and accessibility contract.
- Narrow browsers open expanded and stack contextual and Builder surfaces without requiring the compact chip.
- Conversation tail-follow, stable scroll restoration, keyboard composition, visible focus, reduced motion, and live-region behavior remain intact.

## 9. Ownership and non-owners

| Concern | Owner |
|---|---|
| Shared Corner structure and semantics | `public/index.html` |
| Room-aware and responsive visual hierarchy | `public/styles.css` |
| Projection into the new surfaces | `public/app.js` |
| Live causal state and same-origin transport | existing `public/live-state.js` and `public/corner-api.js`, unchanged unless required by a separately adopted contract |
| Desktop geometry and security | existing `src/corner/` owners, unchanged |

World, rooms, Result Rack, Forest, provider adapters, and Source stores are explicit non-owners of Corner visual organization. The renderer does not reach into their storage or manufacture new domain law.

No persistent-store migration or compatibility facade is required. Existing DOM identifiers and renderer contracts should be preserved where tests, desktop integration, or accessibility depend on them.

## 10. Verification

Focused verification must cover:

- compact/expanded desktop geometry and shared renderer parity;
- truthful location projection plus neutral unknown-location fallback;
- absence of invented movement or capability controls;
- active-lifespan-only conversation and exact safe text rendering;
- causal live cards, approvals, inspection, and Marble Inspector continuity;
- Builder machinery remaining behind an explicit affordance;
- narrow-browser reflow, focus visibility, reduced motion, and scroll restoration; and
- no Faun/unknown identity leakage from visual classification.

The complete suite remains the final regression boundary. Native Electron/tray visual smoke remains separately pending under Corner Desktop v1.
