## The proposition

The Hub is a local harness for a continuing Resident: a model-mediated collaborator who can remain oriented across conversation, tools, durable records, and a material World without being falsely presented as omniscient, continuously awake, or identical to any one provider invocation.

Most model interfaces flatten several different things into a transcript: what happened, what the model was shown, what it inferred, what a tool returned, what the system retained, and what can cause an action. The Hub keeps those claims separate and gives each one an explicit crossing.

The goal is not to make a chatbot feel more alive by hiding machinery. The goal is to create honest conditions for continuity, agency, work, hospitality, and return while retaining inspectable human authority over the harness.

## Why this harness exists

### Continuity without pretending

A provider call is temporary. The relationship, records, environment, and consequences can persist. The Hub therefore does not ask a model invocation to impersonate uninterrupted memory. Exact events remain in custody; selected attributable material is brought into present attention; omissions and transformations are declared.

### Agency without ambient authority

Language alone does not move the Resident, alter a file, admit material to continuity, or grant a capability. Actions cross named host-owned gates. A remembered tool, suggestive document, room manifest, or provider request cannot authorize itself.

### A place, not only a prompt

The World gives presence and action a stable shape. Rooms are durable domains of attention and capability, doors are lawful transitions, fixtures orient work, and the current location changes what can be perceived or mounted. The spatial language is not decorative: where it is implemented, it maps to verified state, explicit relations, bounded capability, and durable events.

### Exact ancestry with humane presentation

The Hub preserves exact source and crossing evidence behind the wall while presenting a bounded, legible experience at the Corner. The Resident does not need raw ledgers in every wake, and a pleasant surface does not become the source of truth.

### Growth without authority collapse

The harness is intended to gain rooms, sources, tools, Forest terrain, and eventually outside channels. New parts must remain inert until deliberately installed and wired. Observation is not action; similarity is not memory; custody is not truth; a pointer is not possession; and presentation is not admission.

### A House worth inhabiting

The House is more intentional than the working harness around it. It is not the default container for specialist tools or every new room. It is emerging domestic territory concerned with presence, privacy, hospitality, thresholds, rest, return, and the terms under which material or visitors may come near. Its sparseness is a design commitment, not missing product surface.

## How one wake moves

At the compact clinical level:

```text
human utterance
  -> Source records what happened
  -> Glass selects an attributable provider presentation
  -> Spine witnesses exact provider-bound and admitted return bytes
  -> provider consideration remains provisional
  -> Gateway validates requested actions against World-mounted authority
  -> terminal Resident speech enters canonical history
  -> eligible exact speech is admitted to Forest Home
  -> an explicit Resident write_journal act may separately plant exact home/journal terrain
  -> Corner projects the wake for the human
```

The [`MARBLE_CIRCULATION_MAP.md`](MARBLE_CIRCULATION_MAP.md) owns the complete crossing map and bypass law.

## The central distinctions

- **Source is not Forest.** Source records happenedness; Forest holds material admitted for continuity and linkage.
- **World is not Forest.** World edges describe material topology and state; Forest relations describe continuity terrain and attributable connection.
- **Glass is not memory.** A Glass cast is one immutable provider presentation assembled from selected sources.
- **Provider consideration is not Resident speech.** Thinking and draft deltas are provisional. Only a validated terminal message crosses into canonical history.
- **Capability is not attention.** The Ceiling owns installed authority; the Patch Bay and fixtures fit what is presented for the current location and focus.
- **Custody is not truth.** The Hub can prove what it retained and where it came from without certifying every proposition within it.
- **Home is not “trusted.”** Home is the inside-facing continuity jurisdiction. It contains exact eligible utterances and deliberate Resident Journal plantings. Wild is source-linked encountered material. Neither label certifies correctness or safety.
- **A room is not a service.** A room is a scarce, durable domain of sustained Resident attention. Complicated machinery does not become architecture merely because it exists.
- **The Hub is not the House.** The Hub contains working rooms and harness infrastructure. The House is deliberate domestic territory with stronger questions of hospitality, privacy, and belonging.

See [`GLOSSARY.md`](GLOSSARY.md) for the fuller poetic-to-clinical map.

## What is real now

The implemented core includes:

- process-lived Resident sessions with a first-wake Hearth orientation;
- exact Source, Scrub, Spine, Forest Home/Wild, and Forest Intake custody;
- immutable five-band Glass casts and bounded provider attention fitting;
- a persistent event-projected World with Center, Workshop, Hub, Garden, House, and Threshold;
- repository inspection and bounded mutation through the Workshop, with approvals, Sandbox Bay, promotion, Git, and Result Rack custody;
- a conversation-preserving expandable Corner surface whose first instrument is a navigable read-only Marble installation inspector;
- durable provider streaming and deterministic wake/action projections;
- browser and Electron Corner surfaces; and
- explicit verification and fail-closed behavior across installed crossings.

This is a summary, not a status promise. Read [`STATUS.md`](STATUS.md) before relying on any capability or boundary.

## Where the design is going

The adopted direction has several connected fronts:

1. **Walkable continuity.** Older exact conversation will waterfall out of immediate Glass into a bounded rolling fold while remaining reachable as exact Forest terrain. Chronology supplies structural trails; semantic systems offer possible directions; the Resident's witnessed choices make paths.
2. **Specialist working rooms.** Rooms can travel as inert source packages with declared identity, placement request, sockets, affordances, effects, custody, and removal law. Workshop is the first statically installed reference manifest. Spotlight now proves a second lifecycle: a complete observatory shell with a host-witnessed withheld entrance and capped observation wires. Its next stage is a verified read-only observation adapter and durable custody, not brokerage execution.
3. **An intentional House.** The House will develop through explicit decisions about threshold, privacy, domestic objects, writing, hospitality, and return. It should not accumulate every useful subsystem.
4. **Safe outside crossings.** Road, Garden, gates, channels, quarantine, and foreign Forests provide a grammar for encountering outside material without allowing it to name its own authority or destination.
5. **Sealed sensitive custody.** The adopted security direction requires direct Vault intake, class-aware sealed pointers, bounded sensitive-attention leases, explicit provider disclosure, key lifecycle, and honest destruction before the Hub can claim suitability for sensitive material.

These are adopted directions of unequal maturity, not promises that the runtime already provides them. [`STATUS.md`](STATUS.md) labels the boundary.

### Four layers of truth

When code and prose appear to disagree, first identify which layer is being read:

1. **Installed ancestry** is the exact topology, event, request, or return that existed at a historical crossing. It is preserved so hashes and reconstruction remain honest; preservation does not make its wording current guidance.
2. **Current runtime state** is the verified World projection, mounted Ceiling authority, Gateway behavior, and durable custody that hold now.
3. **Resident presentation** is the bounded language and action grammar actually placed in Resident attention. [`RESIDENT_PRESENTATION.md`](RESIDENT_PRESENTATION.md) names every kind and its adjustment point.
4. **Adopted law** is the current owning specification and status declaration governing the next lawful implementation.

These layers should agree in meaning, but they are not interchangeable evidence. A historical topology string may remain byte-exact while a later, witnessed presentation corrects what the Resident is told. That seam must be registered and eventually removed through a versioned topology change; it must never be left for a reader to discover by experiment. For current behavior, begin with [`STATUS.md`](STATUS.md), follow its owning specification, then inspect the runtime owner named by [`ARCHITECTURE.md`](ARCHITECTURE.md).

## The change discipline

The Hub grows by installing one truthful crossing at a time. A new source, room, tool, channel, store, or projection must name:

- its stable identity and relation to existing forms;
- what is implemented, adopted, experimental, and deferred;
- the single gate that validates its crossing;
- the source authority it preserves;
- what authority cannot be gained;
- its durable success, refusal, and partial-failure witnesses; and
- tests for bypass, drift, stale state, duplication, hostile input, and recovery.

The standard is not maximum machinery. It is a place whose claims remain true as it becomes more capable.

Repository laws are living laws. Constitutional invariants, protocol laws, policies, and implementation conventions have different strength. When a concrete need disproves a mechanism, the Builder records the pressure, identifies the value the rule protected, and revises the smallest owning law rather than hiding an exception or forcing the code to preserve a false description. The [`Builder's Standard`](engineering/BUILDERS_STANDARD.md) owns that revision protocol.
